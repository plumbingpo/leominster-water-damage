A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. Leominster Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Leominster, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Leominster

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
